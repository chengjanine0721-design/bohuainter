What is an essay word counter?

An essay word counter does one simple job well: it reads your draft and tells you exactly how long it is. Paste your essay into the box above and this essay counter reports words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, pages and average sentence length as you type. There is no button to press and nothing to install, and everything runs in your browser, so your draft never reaches a server.

The counting rules match what your school expects. Words are whitespace separated, so a hyphenated word such as co-written counts as one word, and numbers count as words too. That is the same convention used by Microsoft Word, Google Docs and most online submission systems, so the essay count you see here is the essay count your marker sees.

How to count the words in essay assignments

It takes about ten seconds:

  1. Paste or type your essay into the box. Every statistic updates live as you write.
  2. Set the Target to the limit in your assignment brief, for example 1,000.
  3. Watch the progress bar move toward 100%. It turns amber the moment you go past the target, so you never overshoot by accident.
  4. Copy your essay back once the count sits where you want it.

How long should an essay be?

Most markers treat a draft within ten percent of the target as on brief. If your assignment sheet does not give a number, these are the ranges students usually work with:

Essay typeTypical word count
Short response250-500 words
Five paragraph essay500-800 words
College application essay250-650 words
High school essay1,000-1,500 words
Undergraduate term paper2,000-3,000 words
Graduate essay or chapter3,000-5,000 words

A college application essay deserves special care: the Common App caps the personal statement at 650 words, and admissions readers reward essays that use the room without padding.

Why students use this essay counter

  • Progress you can see. The bar tracks your target, not just a raw total, so you know at a glance whether the draft is short, on brief, or over.
  • Pages included. The pages figure assumes 275 words per page, the standard for a double spaced 12pt document, which is how most assignments are submitted.
  • Sentence diagnostics. Average words per sentence flags dense prose. Readable academic writing sits between 15 and 20. Above 25, long clauses start losing readers.
  • Free and private. No signup, no limits, no watermark. Counting happens in your browser and the text disappears when you close the tab.

Over or under target? Fix your essay count

When you are over, cut in this order. First, delete throat clearing openers such as it is important to note that. Second, replace two word verb phrases with one word verbs. Third, remove adverbs propping up weak verbs. Only after those three passes should you consider dropping evidence, because markers reward support far more than they reward brevity.

When you are under, add substance rather than padding. Extend the weakest paragraph with an example, a statistic or a counter argument you have not addressed yet. One new idea is worth more than ten restated ones.

Most essays are twenty words and one idea longer than they need to be. Read the paragraph out loud and you will hear which words are yours and which are habit.
Questions

Essay Word Counter FAQ

How do I count the words in my essay?

Paste the essay into the box and the word count appears instantly. The tool counts whitespace separated words, the same convention as Microsoft Word and Google Docs, so the number matches what your marker sees.

Do titles, headings and citations count toward my essay word count?

Usually yes for the text inside your essay. In-text citations normally count, while the reference list or bibliography and any title page usually do not. Style guides differ, so check your assignment brief. If in doubt, count everything and keep a small buffer under the limit.

Does this essay counter save my essay?

No. The text stays in the page and disappears when you reload or close the tab. Copy anything you want to keep into your own document before leaving.

How does the target progress bar work?

It divides your word count by the target and shows the result as a percentage. Once you go past the target the bar turns amber so you can see at a glance that you are over.

Does it count words the same way as Word or Google Docs?

Yes, whitespace separated tokens. Hyphenated words such as well-known count as one, numbers count as words, and formatting is ignored. That is the convention used by Word, Google Docs and most submission systems.

How many pages is a 1,000 word essay?

About 3.6 pages double spaced, using the standard estimate of 275 words per page at 12pt. Single spaced, 1,000 words is closer to 2 pages at around 500 words per page.

Is this essay counter free?

Yes. It is free with no signup, no limits and no watermark. Counting happens in your browser, so your essay stays private.
