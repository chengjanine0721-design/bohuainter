What is a word frequency counter?

A word frequency counter tells you which words appear most often in a piece of text. Paste an article, an essay or a paragraph and it splits the text into words, normalizes them and ranks them by how many times each one occurs. The result is a clear picture of the vocabulary you actually used, not the vocabulary you meant to use.

The tool is deliberately small. It gives you the top eight words as chips and a handful of word statistics, so you can judge repetition without opening a spreadsheet. Everything happens in the browser, which means a private draft stays private.

How the counter reads your text

Three steps turn raw text into a word frequency count. First the text is split on whitespace, so a word is any run of characters between spaces. Second every word is lowercased, which makes The and the identical. Third punctuation is stripped from the edges of each word, so a full stop or a comma at the end no longer creates a separate token. The remaining words are tallied and sorted, highest first.

This is a simple, predictable rule set. It does not try to guess that run and running are the same word. They are counted separately, which is what you want when you are checking literal repetition rather than stems.

Reading the top eight words

The eight most common words appear as chips under the text, each with its number beside it. That is the fastest way to answer how frequent is this word in your draft. If a word you care about is missing from the chips, its count sits below the eighth ranked word, and the ranking tells you roughly how far below.

Word order does not matter. The counter looks at the whole passage at once, so the same word at the start and the end of a text is added together. Short common words such as the and a usually top the list, which is expected in english word frequency results.

Word frequency in English

English word frequency has a familiar shape. Function words like the, of, and and to tend to lead any list, followed by the nouns and verbs that carry the topic. Seeing that shape in your own text is useful. If your article is about a single subject and the top words are all generic, the topic words are not landing.

Word frequency english text is the natural fit for this tool because the rules match how English is written, with spaces between words and punctuation attached to the edges. Texts without spaces, or with punctuation used inside words, need a different approach.

The pattern is consistency. The counter changes only case and edge punctuation. It never merges two different spellings and never splits one typed word, so the number you see is a literal count you can reproduce by hand.

Uses for a frequency word count

A frequency word count has practical uses. Editors use it to catch a word that appears too often and needs a synonym. Students use it to check keyword balance in an essay. Marketers use a frequency word counter to see whether a page repeats a phrase the right number of times without stuffing it.

Because the tool also reports unique words, the longest word and average word length, it doubles as a light word frequency analyzer. Those word statistics describe the texture of a text, from short and plain to long and technical, and they update as you type.

Count frequency of words by hand and by tool

You can count frequency of words with a pen, a sorted list and a lot of patience. The tool does the same job in a moment and does not lose your place. Paste the text once, then read the chips and the summary numbers together. The chips show what repeats, and the summary shows the size and shape of the whole passage.

Nothing is saved between visits. Refresh the page and the text is gone, which is the same privacy rule used across the site. If you want to keep the result, note the numbers down before you leave.

Questions

Word Frequency Counter FAQ

How does this word frequency counter work?

Paste text and the tool splits it on whitespace, lowercases every token and strips punctuation from the edges. It then counts each word and shows the eight most common ones as chips, alongside the total words, unique words and average word length.

What counts as a single word?

A word is any run of characters between spaces. Case is ignored, so The and the are the same word, and edge punctuation is removed, so end. and end both count as end. Hyphenated words stay as written.

How do I count frequency of words in a long document?

Paste the whole document into the box. The word occurrence count updates as you type and ranks every term, so the words you lean on most rise to the top without any manual tally.

What is a word occurrence count?

It is the number of times one exact word appears after normalization. If the word the shows up forty times in your text, its word occurrence count is forty, whether it was written as the or The.

Does this work as an english word frequency tool?

Yes. Because tokens are lowercased and punctuation is stripped, the tool handles english word frequency well, including contractions and words at the end of sentences. Word frequency english text is the main use case.

What is a frequency word counter used for?

Writers use a frequency word counter to spot repeated words, check keyword balance in an article and find filler. A frequency word count also reveals which terms dominate a draft, which is useful when you are editing for variety.

Is this a word frequency analyzer?

It is a simple word frequency analyzer. Beyond ranking words it reports word statistics such as unique words, the longest word and average word length, enough to describe a text without exporting it to a spreadsheet.

How frequent is this particular word in my text?

Read the top eight chips. If your word is not among them, its count is lower than the eighth ranked word. The chips show each word with its number so you can see how frequent is this term at a glance.
