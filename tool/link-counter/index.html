What a link counter does

A link counter answers three questions at once. How many links are in this list. How many of them are unique. And how long is each one. Paste one URL per line and the tool reports totals, distinct domains, average length and every link that runs past the recommended limit, with the longest one called out separately.

That is useful for an outreach list, a sitemap draft, a resource page or a footer that has grown quietly for three years. Length is the part most people never check, and it is the part that decides whether a reader sees the whole URL or a truncated stub.

How to check URL length

Paste the URL on its own line and read the length column in the list below the counter. Anything between 60 and 75 characters is comfortable. Between 75 and 115 the link will still work but search results and social cards will cut it. Past 115, expect wrapping and broken previews, and past 2,000 you are testing a technical limit rather than a design choice.

Counting includes the protocol, the domain and every slash. That is the honest number, because it is what a browser and a search result handle. If you want to judge the readable part only, count from the first slash after the domain.

The gap between the technical ceiling and the practical one is the whole story. A URL can be two thousand characters and still be a bad URL, because nobody sees the end of it.

SEO URL length and why short wins

Short URLs win for reasons that have nothing to do with ranking algorithms. They are easier to read in a result, easier to remember, easier to say out loud and easier to type from a screenshot. They also tend to be more stable, because a short path usually names a topic while a long path names a database query.

A good URL answers what the page is in three or four words. A bad one carries a date, a category, a subcategory, a slug and four tracking parameters, and the only part a reader needs is the slug. When a link must carry tracking, keep the clean URL for publication and use the tagged version in the specific campaign only.

Counting links in a document or a page

The count matters where link volume is a signal of quality. A resource page with 200 outbound links is a list, not a recommendation, and readers treat it that way. A footer with 60 links dilutes every one of them. The unique figure is the useful one for auditing: twenty occurrences of the same three links is a layout decision, while twenty distinct domains is a curated list.

Domains give the third view. A page linking to twelve different domains reads as research. A page linking to twelve pages of one domain reads as promotion, which is fine when that is the intent and a problem when it is not.

URL length rules worth keeping

Lowercase, hyphens rather than underscores, no stop words, no dates unless the content is dated, no more than three path levels and no parameter that a path could replace. Those six rules cover almost every URL problem the length check will find, and they are cheaper to apply when a page is created than when it is being fixed two years later.

If a URL has to change, a permanent redirect preserves most of what the old one earned. What it does not preserve is every link that pointed at the old address and never followed the redirect, which is why the cheapest fix is still the one made before publication.

URL length questions in short

  • Max URL length for a search result is about 75 characters before Google truncates the line.
  • URL count is the links figure, and the unique figure tells you how many of them are duplicates.
  • Maximum size of URL inside a sitemap entry is 2,048 characters.
  • URL length limit in older browsers sat near 2,083 characters, far past anything readable.
  • URL counter is the shorter name for this page. It measures length instead of fetching pages.
A URL that needs to be explained is usually a URL that was designed for a database instead of a reader.
Questions

Link Counter FAQ

How long can a URL be?

Technically about 2,083 characters in many browsers and 2,048 in a sitemap entry. Practically, keep it under 75 characters. Anything past that is cut off in search results and in most social previews.

What is the maximum URL length for SEO?

There is no ranking rule, but there is a display rule. Google shows roughly 75 characters of a URL in a result, so the part that identifies the page should arrive before that point.

How do I count the links in a document?

Paste one URL per line and the counter reports total links, unique links, distinct domains and the number that exceed the recommended length. Duplicates are counted once in the unique figure, so a repeated footer link does not inflate the total.

Does the tool check whether a link works?

No. It measures length and counts links, which needs no network access and works for private or staging URLs. Use a link checker for status codes, and this tool for length and quantity.

Do tracking parameters make a URL too long?

Often yes. Parameters such as utm source and utm campaign can double the length of a short path, and they also create duplicate URLs that split signals. Keep tracking in tags where the platform supports them rather than in the link you publish.

What counts as a long URL?

Over 75 characters is where search results start cutting. Over 115 is where many social cards and email clients break the link across lines. Over 2,000 is a technical limit rather than a design one.

Should a URL use hyphens or underscores?

Hyphens. Search engines read hyphens as word separators and do not treat underscores the same way. Keep the URL lowercase, drop stop words and avoid dates unless the content is genuinely dated.
