ModeWhat it doesExample

Questions

Upper Case Generator FAQ

Does the conversion keep my paragraphs?

Yes. Line breaks, spaces and punctuation are preserved exactly. Only the case of the letters changes.

Is there a length limit?

No limit is enforced. The text is processed in your browser, so a few hundred thousand characters will still convert instantly.

Which should I use for headings?

Title case is the common convention for headings and buttons on the web. Sentence case is the convention in many style guides for article titles and body copy.
