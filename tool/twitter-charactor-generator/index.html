Twitter X Character Limit: Every Surface Explained

The number most people mean when they say the limit is 280. That replaced the original 140 in November 2017, when the company doubled the cap after finding that users kept running into it. The 140 figure never fully disappeared. Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters count as two units each, so 140 of them fill the same space as 280 Latin letters.

Every place you can type on X has its own ceiling, and they are not all the same number. An X post length checker is only useful if it knows which one you are writing for. The table below lists the surfaces that catch people out.

SurfaceLimitWhat to know
Tweet / post280Standard free account. Every post gets its own 280.
Reply280The @handle you are replying to does not count.
Thread7,84028 connected posts at 280 each before you start a new thread.
Direct message10,000Per message. Long enough for a full pitch.
Profile name50The display name above your handle. Emoji allowed.
Twitter handle15Letters, numbers and underscores only.
Twitter bio160Same cap as an old SMS segment. Tight, so plan for it.
Premium post25,000Paid accounts only. The timeline shows the first 280 with "show more".

Do spaces count toward the limit

Yes. Every space is one unit, including the double space some people leave after a full stop and the indents at the start of a line. A paragraph break costs two units, one for each line break. Trimming double spaces is the fastest way to reclaim room in a post that is just over.

Do hashtags count toward the limit

Yes, and there is no free hashtag allowance. A hashtag is charged one unit per visible character including the # symbol, so #food is five units and #contentmarketing is seventeen. Three mid-length hashtags can eat a tenth of a standard post before you have written a sentence.

What the Premium limit adds

A Premium account raises the ceiling on a single post from 280 to 25,000. The post still shows the first couple of sentences in the timeline with a "show more" link, so the extra room is suited to essays, announcements and anything that would otherwise become a long thread. Replies and quote posts on Premium can also run past 280.

How Twitter Counts Characters

X does not count characters the way a plain text editor does. A handful of rules differ, and each one changes what a draft actually costs. These rules catch people out because the visible length of a post and the number X charges can be far apart.

What you typeWhat X chargesWhy
A link23 unitsEvery URL is rewritten to a t.co short link. A 12 character link and a 200 character link both cost 23.
An emoji2 unitsMost single emoji are stored as a UTF-16 surrogate pair. Combined emoji such as flags, skin tone variants and family groupings can run to 4, 7 or 11.
Chinese, Japanese, Korean2 unitsThese scripts carry roughly twice the meaning per glyph, which is why the Japanese version of the site spent years on a 140 cap.
Arabic, Hebrew1 unitRight to left scripts count per character, like Latin text.
A line break1 unitA blank line between paragraphs costs two.
@mentions and #hashtags1 per characterThe @ and # symbols count. There is no free space for either.

This X character counter applies all of those rules while you type. Counted units is the figure X will enforce. Raw characters is the plain length of the string, which is useful when a post is mostly links and you want to see how far the two numbers drift apart. If they disagree sharply, a link was probably written without its https:// or www. prefix and the counter could not spot it.

One thing to keep in mind about combined emoji. A flag, a family or a thumbs up with a skin tone is built from several code points joined together, so the real cost is higher than the flat 2 units this counter charges. Chaining four or five of them in one post can push a draft over the limit without the number moving much. Check the final draft inside X before a post that leans on them.

Twitter Character Counter FAQ

Is Twitter still 140 characters?

No. The cap moved from 140 to 280 in November 2017 for Latin scripts. The old number still shapes how much text a post holds, because Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters are charged at two units each, so a post in those scripts fits roughly 140 characters into the same 280 units.

What is the Twitter X character limit in 2026, and what was the old limit?

280 characters per post on a standard (free) account. 25,000 characters on Premium accounts. A single thread can hold up to 28 connected tweets, so 28 x 280 = 7,840 characters before you have to start a new thread. The old limit was 140, which held from 2006 until late 2017.

What is the 4-1-1 rule on Twitter?

It is a content mix, not a character rule. For every six posts, share four pieces of useful content written by other people, one repost or personal update, and one post that promotes your own work. Andrew Davis proposed it at Tippingpoint Labs and Joe Pulizzi helped spread it. Treat it as a balance check rather than a quota.

How do I post a tweet longer than 280 characters?

Three routes. Start a thread, which allows up to 28 connected posts at 280 each. Subscribe to Premium, which lifts a single post to 25,000 characters. Or move the text into an image, a screenshot or a link to a longer article. For a free account the thread is the practical option.

Are hashtags included in Twitter character limits?

Yes, at one unit per visible character including the # symbol. #food is five units and #contentmarketing is seventeen. There is no free hashtag allowance, so three long tags can cost you a tenth of a standard post before the first sentence.

Do emoji count as 1 or 2 characters?

Two for most single emoji, because X stores them as a UTF-16 surrogate pair. Combined emoji cost more. A flag, a skin tone variant or a family grouping can run to 4, 7 or 11 units. If a draft leans on those, check the final count inside X before publishing.

Do spaces count as characters on Twitter?

Yes. Every space is one unit, including double spaces and the indent at the start of a line. A blank line between paragraphs costs two, one for each line break. Removing stray double spaces is the quickest way to pull a post back under the cap.

Is the Twitter character counter free?

Yes. It is a free character counter with no signup, no paywall and no cap on how much you paste in. Counting runs in your browser, so the text never leaves your device and nothing is stored after you close the tab.
