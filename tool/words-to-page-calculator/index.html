How words translate into pages

The conversion is not fixed. It depends on four settings most people never think about: font and point size, line spacing, margins and the page size itself. A page of 12pt Times New Roman double spaced with one inch margins holds roughly 250 to 300 words. Switch to single spacing and the same page holds about 500. Drop the margins to half an inch and you gain another hundred.

Because of this, the only honest answer to how many pages is 1,000 words is another question: in what format? Academic submission guidelines usually specify all four settings, and the page count follows from there.

Does the printed sheet count differ

Yes. One sheet of paper printed on both sides carries two pages. Students submitting a thesis are often told a length in pages per side, so the printed sheets figure here doubles the page count when you pick double spacing. Check which one your submission actually asks for.

One honest caveat about page count

Word processors do not lay text out identically. Headings, footnotes, block quotes and figures all break the rhythm, and a paragraph that starts halfway down a page wastes the remainder. Expect a real document to run about five percent longer than the arithmetic suggests.

If a word limit matters more than a page limit, always write to the word count. Pages are an artefact of formatting, words are the substance.
Questions

Words to Pages Calculator FAQ

How many pages is 1,000 words

About four pages double spaced and two pages single spaced with standard 12pt formatting. The exact figure moves with font, margins and whether you count headings.

Is a 500 word essay one page

Single spaced, yes, roughly. Double spaced it is closer to two pages. Most teachers who ask for one page double spaced expect 250 to 300 words.

How many words are on a page of a novel

Published paperbacks hold 250 to 300 words per page on average. Word processors set to manuscript format also land near 250 words per page because of double spacing.

Why do different calculators give different answers

They assume different words per page. This tool shows the assumption and lets you change it, which is more useful than a single fixed number.
