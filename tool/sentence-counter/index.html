What is a sentence counter?

A sentence counter tells you how many sentences a piece of text contains. Paste a paragraph and it scans the text for sentence endings, then reports the sentence count together with the word total, average words per sentence, the longest sentence and an estimated reading time. It is a quick way to check the shape of a draft before you edit it.

The numbers work together. A high count with a low average means many short sentences. A low count with a high average means fewer, longer ones. Neither is right or wrong on its own, but the pair describes the rhythm of your writing better than a single figure.

How the counter finds sentences

The rule is deliberately plain. A sentence ends at a full stop, a question mark or an exclamation mark when that mark is followed by whitespace or the end of the text. Everything before the next ending counts as one sentence. This is why the tool can count sentences without understanding grammar, and why it runs entirely in your browser.

The trade off is that abbreviations with a full stop, such as Dr., can look like an ending to the tool. If your text is dense with initials or abbreviations, read the result once. For ordinary prose, the rule is reliable and fast.

Sentence length and readability

Average words per sentence is the most useful number for readability. Short sentences carry one idea and are easy to scan on a phone. Long sentences can hold more detail but ask more of the reader. A mix usually reads best, with a few long sentences broken up by short ones.

The longest sentence figure is the other signal worth watching. When it runs far above the average, it is often a sentence that grew by accretion and now carries two or three ideas. That is a good place to start editing, because splitting one long sentence usually improves more than trimming ten short ones.

Sentence guidance by context

ContextSentence guidance
Plain English writingaim for 15 to 20 words per sentence
Academic prose20 to 25 words is common
Web copy and landing pagesunder 20 words reads best
Mobile screensshorter sentences avoid wrapping
Social postsone idea per sentence
Technical documentationone instruction per sentence
Writing for children10 to 15 words per sentence

These are conventions, not laws. The value of the table is that it gives you a target to compare against your own average. If you write web copy and your average is thirty words, you already know which way to edit.

Reading time and word count

Reading time is estimated from the word total at a typical adult reading pace. It is a planning figure, useful when you are deciding whether a piece belongs in a short email or a long article. Treat it as a rough guide rather than a promise about any one reader.

The word total behind it is counted the same way as elsewhere on the site, by splitting on whitespace. That means the number you see here matches the word count you would get from a plain word counter, so you can count sentences and check length in one pass.

How to write clearer sentences

Start with one idea per sentence. Put the main point near the front, then add the detail. If you find yourself joining clauses with several commas, consider two sentences instead. Then look at how many sentences you have. If the answer to how many sentences is very low for a long paragraph, the ideas are probably bunched together.

Reading aloud still beats any rule. A sentence you can say in one breath is usually fine. A sentence that leaves you short of breath needs a full stop somewhere in the middle. The counter confirms what your ear already heard.

Questions

Sentence Counter FAQ

How does this sentence counter work?

The tool reads your text and ends a sentence at a full stop, a question mark or an exclamation mark when it is followed by a space or the end of the text. It then reports the number of sentences, total words, average words per sentence, the longest sentence and an estimated reading time.

How many sentences are in my paragraph?

Paste the paragraph into the box and the number appears at once. The sentence count updates as you type, so you can add or remove a sentence and watch the figure change without pressing a button.

How do I count sentences in a long document?

Paste the whole document. A sentence counter online handles a single line or a full page the same way, scanning all the text and splitting on terminal punctuation. Longer text simply gives a higher count.

What counts as one sentence?

A sentence is a run of text that ends when a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark is followed by whitespace or the end of the input. That rule is simple and fast, which is why the sentence count is predictable.

What about abbreviations such as Dr. or e.g.?

Because a full stop followed by a space ends a sentence, an abbreviation like Dr. can be read as a sentence end. The same is true of initials. Proofread the result once if your text is full of abbreviations, since no simple sentence counter can tell Dr. Smith from a real stop.

What does average words per sentence tell me?

It is a quick readability signal. A low number means short, punchy sentences. A high number means long sentences that may be hard to follow. Many writing guides aim for an average of around fifteen to twenty words, and the counter shows where your text sits.

How is the reading time estimated?

Reading time is worked out from the word count at a typical adult reading speed. It is a rough guide for planning, not a precise measurement, but it is handy when you need to know whether a piece is a one minute read or a five minute one.
