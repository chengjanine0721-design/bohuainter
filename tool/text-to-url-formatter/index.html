What is a text to url formatter?

A text to url formatter takes three ordinary pieces of information and produces one precise link. The first piece is your main domain, such as https://bohuainter.com. The second is the subpage that the link lives under, such as tool or blog. The third is the link text itself, the phrase that describes the page. The formatter lowercases that phrase, strips anything a URL cannot carry, joins the remaining words with single hyphens and attaches the result to the domain and path. What you get is a clean address you can paste into an article, a sitemap or a campaign.

The conversion matters because links are unforgiving. A URL with a capital letter, a space or a stray symbol still works in many browsers, but it looks untrusted, it breaks some tools and it is harder to read out loud. Running your text in link form through a formatter removes that whole class of problems before the link is ever published.

How to make text into link form step by step

Searches like how to make text into link usually describe the same job this page does, and the steps take a few seconds. Type your domain in the first field. If you leave off the https part, the formatter adds it for you. Type the subpage in the second field. A single level such as tool is enough, and deeper paths such as docs/guides work too, because every level is cleaned separately. Then type the words that name your page in the third field.

The formatted URL updates as you type, and the figures below it tell you whether the result is ready. When the Ready figure says Yes, press Copy link and the full address is on your clipboard. That is all it takes to convert to link form: three short fields, one button and no markup to write by hand.

The rules are deliberately plain. Everything is lowercased. Every run of characters that a URL cannot carry, including spaces, punctuation and symbols, becomes a single hyphen. Hyphens at the start or end are removed, and repeated hyphens collapse into one. The words themselves are never reordered or dropped, so the slug still reads like the phrase you typed.

How to turn a link into text

The reverse question comes up just as often, and the answer is already on the screen. To turn a link into text, read the slug backwards. Replace every hyphen with a space and you have the phrase again. A link ending in text-to-url-formatter reads as text to url formatter, which is why hyphenated slugs are the ones search engines and readers both prefer. Underscores and glued words do not give you that reading back, which is a quiet reason to keep your slugs hyphenated.

Reading a link aloud before you publish is the fastest check. If the spoken version sounds like a sentence fragment that names the page, the link is doing its job. If it sounds like a code fragment, retype the phrase in the third field and let the formatter rebuild it.

Convert to link and check the result

The figures under the panel are there so the conversion can be checked, not just produced. URL characters shows the full length of the finished address, which matters because extremely long URLs get truncated in some tools. Slug characters and Words in slug show whether the slug is tight. Three to five words is a good range for most pages. Path segments counts the levels between the domain and the page, and Domain length shows the size of the host itself.

When a URL comes out longer than you expected, shorten the link text rather than the domain. When it comes out shorter than expected, check that you typed the whole phrase. The formatter only joins what you give it, and the numbers make that contract visible.

Questions

Text to URL Formatter FAQ

What is a text to url formatter?

A text to url formatter turns ordinary words into a working link. You give it a domain, a subpage path and a line of text, and it lowercases the words, joins them with hyphens and attaches them to your domain as a clean URL you can copy.

How do I make text into link form with this page?

Type your domain in the first field, the subpage in the second and your words in the third. The formatted URL appears as you type. Press Copy link and the full URL is on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

How do I turn a link into text?

Read the slug backwards. Replace every hyphen with a space and capitalise the words you need. A URL like bohuainter.com/tool/text-to-url-formatter/ reads as text to url formatter, which is exactly the phrase behind it.

What makes a good slug?

Keep it lowercase, separate words with single hyphens and cut filler words. Three to five meaningful words describe a page well. The formatter does the joining for you, so you only need to pick the right words.

Does the formatter upload my text anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The domain, the subpage and the link text never leave the page, so an unpublished project stays private.

Can I build links several levels deep?

Yes. Type a path such as blog or docs/guides in the subpage field and the formatter keeps the levels. Each level is slugified, so mixed case and spaces are corrected on the way in.
