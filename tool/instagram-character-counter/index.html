What are the Instagram character limits?

Instagram does not use one limit. Each field has its own budget, and the four that people write in most often are the caption, the comment, the bio and the alt text. A caption allows 2,200 characters, a comment allows the same, a bio stops at 150 and alt text stops at 100. A name field and a username each allow 30, and a direct message allows 1,000.

That spread is why a single number is not enough. A paragraph that fits a caption perfectly is four times too long for a bio, and a line that looks fine in a comment can be five times over the alt text limit. The Instagram character counter above keeps the target in view, so the remaining figure always answers the question you actually asked.

Why the 125 character cutoff matters more than 2,200

A caption survives the limit long before it survives the feed. Instagram shows roughly the first 125 characters before it folds the rest behind a more link, and on a phone that is about two lines of text. The 2,200 limit is a ceiling. The 125 character preview is the part that decides whether anyone taps.

Write the hook first and count it. If the opening line needs the second sentence to make sense, it is not a hook yet. Front load the specific detail, the number, the contradiction or the question, then let the rest of the caption pay it off. The counter shows characters, so you can watch the preview boundary arrive instead of guessing.

How to use this Instagram character counter

It takes a few seconds:

  1. Pick the field you are writing for. Caption, comment, bio and alt text each load their own limit.
  2. Paste or type the text. Characters, words, hashtags, mentions and lines update as you go.
  3. Watch the bar and the remaining figure. When the count passes the limit the bar turns amber and the remaining figure goes negative.
  4. Copy the text back into Instagram once the count sits where you want it.

The sample button loads a short caption with a hook, a line break, two hashtags and one mention, so you can see every figure move before you paste your own copy.

Word count and hashtags inside a caption

Character count answers the platform question. Word count answers the writing question. A caption of 2,200 characters can be 350 words of dense copy, which nobody reads on a phone, or 60 words with line breaks, which reads comfortably. The words box gives you the second number so you can judge pace rather than just fit.

Hashtags count as words because they have no spaces, and the hashtag counter keeps a separate tally. Mentions work the same way. If you are writing a Reel script rather than a caption, the same boxes apply: keep the spoken part under about 150 words for a 60 second Reel and put the detail in the caption instead.

Bio, alt text and comments: the short fields

The 150 character bio is the hardest field on Instagram. One line of positioning, one line of proof, and a link. The counter helps because it forces a decision: if the second line does not add information, it is costing characters that the first line could use.

Alt text is capped at 100 characters, which is roughly one clear sentence. Describe what the image shows and why it is there, skip the words image and photo, and leave the hook to the caption. Comments are capped at 2,200, plenty for a real reply and far too much for the comment that just says fire emoji.

Questions

Instagram Character Counter FAQ

What is the character limit on Instagram?

A caption or Reel caption allows 2,200 characters, a comment allows 2,200, a bio allows 150, a name field allows 30 and alt text allows 100. The chips above switch between those limits, so the remaining figure always refers to the field you are writing.

What is the Instagram bio character limit?

150 characters. The bio cuts off with no warning when you paste something longer, so it is worth checking before you save. Anything creative you want to say about the account has to fit in one short line, which is why most bios use a line break and an emoji instead of a sentence.

Is there a character limit on Instagram posts?

Yes, 2,200 characters for the caption of a feed post or a Reel. Only the first 125 characters show before the more link, so the limit is rarely the problem. Being buried behind the more link is.

How many hashtags can I use on Instagram?

Instagram accepts up to 30 hashtags per post and per comment. Most accounts in 2026 use 3 to 5 specific tags rather than thirty generic ones, because the caption text carries the topic signal and the tags mostly help with grouping.

Does an emoji count as one character?

Usually yes. Instagram measures characters rather than bytes, so a single emoji counts as one even though it is stored as several bytes. Emoji built from several people, or with a skin tone modifier, can count as more than one.

How do I get a word count for an Instagram caption?

Paste the caption into the box and read the words figure. Words are counted as whitespace separated tokens, so a hashtag counts as one word and so does a handle. That matches how a script or a shot list would count the same text.

Do line breaks and spaces count toward the limit?

Yes. Every space and every line break is a character. Captions that use one sentence per line look tidy and cost a few characters per break, which matters when a bio is capped at 150.
