How Many Words Is a Typical Novel

Genres differ more than most guides admit. Here are real ranges for debut and established novels by category.

2026-10-07·7 min read·Bohuainter team

The short answer is 80,000 to 100,000 words for adult fiction. The useful answer is that the range swings by more than a hundred thousand words depending on genre, and publishers care far more about genre convention than about any universal target.

Word counts by genre

CategoryTypical rangeSweet spot
Literary fiction70,000 to 120,00085,000
Commercial thriller70,000 to 100,00085,000
Romance50,000 to 90,00070,000
Mystery and crime70,000 to 90,00080,000

Memoir70,000 to 100,00085,000
Business and self help40,000 to 70,00055,000

Why fantasy runs long

The genre needs room for a world before it needs room for a plot. Maps, magic systems, invented history and the reader's gradual orientation all consume pages. Readers of the category actively want length, which is why a 150,000 word epic fantasy can sell while a 150,000 word thriller gets rejected.

The debut penalty

A first novel faces a harder word count ceiling than an established one. Publishers take a risk on an unknown writer, and a very long manuscript costs more to edit, print and launch. An agent who reads 80,000 words and loves them can imagine the book in shops. An agent staring at 240,000 words from an unknown name sees an editing bill.

The practical ceiling for a debut in most adult genres is around 100,000 to 110,000 words. Above that, structural cuts usually make the book stronger anyway.

Words per page, for the physical sense

A paperback page holds roughly 250 to 300 words. That puts an 80,000 word novel near 300 pages and a 120,000 word novel near 420. If you want a physical target, think in pages, then count words to see where you land.

Children's books and novellas

Middle grade sits between 30,000 and 55,000 words. Early readers and chapter books are far shorter, from a few hundred words up to 15,000. A novella, a category without a formal definition, is generally 20,000 to 40,000 words.

Write the book your story needs, then trim it to the upper edge of your genre's range. Nobody has ever complained that a good novel was slightly too short.
