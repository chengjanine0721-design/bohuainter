How Do You Get Word Count on Pages: Apple Devices and More

Where the Pages word counter lives on Mac, iPhone and iPad, and when a free online counter is the faster way to check.

Checking word count is a routine part of writing assignments, articles, and other documents. In a Reddit discussion, a freelance writer using a MacBook asked how to check the word count in Apple Pages after struggling to find the feature. Apple Pages includes a built-in word counter, but its display setting is easy to overlook, prompting searches for get word count on pages.

The steps for displaying the count differ slightly on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. This guide explains how to find the word count on each device and when an online word counter is useful for checking text from other sources or viewing additional writing statistics.

Finding Word Count in Apple Pages

Apple Pages has a built-in word counter for tracking document length while writing. In addition to words, it can display characters, paragraphs, and pages. However, the counter may need to be enabled before these statistics appear. The steps below explain how to find word count in Pages across Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Finding Word Count on Pages for Mac

Once enabled, the word count remains visible at the bottom of the Pages window and updates as the document changes. The counter can also be dragged to a different position within the window, making it easier to keep track of document length while writing. Its drop-down menu switches between words, characters with or without spaces, paragraphs, and pages. The counter stays available until it is manually hidden or the document window is closed.

Step 1. Open the document in Apple Pages.

Step 2. Click View in the toolbar and select Show Word Count.

Step 3. Check the counter at the bottom of the window. To view another statistic, click the arrows beside the count and select the preferred option.

Step 4. To hide the counter, click View again and select Hide Word Count. The menu label may change to match the statistic currently displayed, such as Hide Character Count.

Viewing Word Count on Pages on iPhone and iPad

On iPhone and iPad, the word count appears near the bottom of the screen after the feature is enabled. The counter can be dragged to a screen corner to keep it visible without taking up as much space. Tapping it opens a list of text statistics, including word count, character count, paragraph count, and page count. This makes it possible to check document length and other text details while editing on a mobile device.

On iPhone:

Step 1. Open the document in the Pages app.

Step 2. Tap the More button (three dots) at the top of the screen and select View Options.

Step 3. Tap Show Word Count to display the counter near the bottom of the screen.

Step 4. Tap the counter to choose another statistic, such as characters with or without spaces, paragraphs, or pages.

On iPad:

Step 1. Open the document in the Pages app.

Step 2. Tap View Options in the toolbar and select Show Word Count.

Step 3. Tap the counter at the bottom of the screen to switch between the available statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Pages Count Only Selected Text?

Yes. Pages can count a selected portion of a document instead of the full text. Highlight the paragraph or passage you want to check, and the counter updates to show statistics for that selection. To return to the document total, clear the selection by clicking or tapping elsewhere in the document.

Why Does Pages Show Character Count Instead of Word Count?

Pages may be showing characters with spaces or characters without spaces because the counter was switched to a different statistic. Open the counter menu and choose words. On Mac, click the arrows beside the counter, and on iPhone or iPad, tap the counter to view the available options.

Why Is the Word Count Missing in Pages?

Anyone wondering how to see word count on pages when the counter is missing should first check whether it is turned off. On Mac, open View and select Show Word Count. On the iPhone, tap More, select View Options, then tap Show Word Count. On iPad, open View Options and select Show Word Count.
