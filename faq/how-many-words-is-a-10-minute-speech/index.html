How Many Words Is a 10 Minute Speech

1,300 to 1,500 words at a normal pace, or 1,400 as a working target. Rates by delivery style, a table for 2, 4, 8 and 15 minutes, and how to time a draft.

A ten minute speech runs about 1,300 to 1,500 words at a normal speaking pace of 130 to 150 words per minute. Plan for 1,400 words and keep a little slack for pauses, a laugh, a sip of water and the moment you look up from the page. If you speak quickly or the room is large, aim for the lower end of the range.

How many words are in a 10 minute speech

Ten minutes multiplied by 140 words per minute gives 1,400 words. The range exists because nobody speaks at a constant rate. A speaker who pauses for effect, waits for applause or slows down for a technical point can lose 200 words of content in ten minutes without noticing. A nervous speaker reading quickly can add the same amount.

The same arithmetic answers how many words in a ten minute speech for almost any setting, since ten minutes is ten minutes whether the room holds ten people or a thousand. What changes is the pace, and with it the number you should write.

Speech coaches usually plan at 130 words per minute for a formal talk and 150 for a relaxed one. Anything outside that band either sounds rushed or starts to drag, which is the opposite of what a tight ten minute slot needs.

Two things change as the slot gets longer. Pauses become a bigger share of the time, so a twenty minute talk usually needs fewer words per minute than a five minute one. And a longer slot invites structure, which means transitions, which cost time without adding word count.

How many words for a 2 minute speech

About 260 to 300 words, which is the answer both to how many words for a 2 minute speech and to how many words in a 2 minute speech. That is a single story or a single argument, not a small version of a full talk. At this length, cut the introduction to one sentence and end on the point rather than summarising it.

How many words is a 4 minute speech

About 520 to 600 words. Four minutes is enough for a short anecdote plus a claim plus one piece of evidence. Most conference lightning talks sit here, and most of them run over because the speaker writes 800 words and hopes to speed up.

How many words is an 8 minute speech

About 1,040 to 1,200 words. Eight minutes comfortably holds three points with one example each, and starts to feel crowded at five points. People planning a pitch often arrive at this length through the query how many words is a 8 minute speech, and the answer is the same range with the lower end as the safer target.

Eight minutes is also the length where structure starts to matter more than wording. Three points with an example each fits comfortably. Five points does not, no matter how short each one is.

How many words is a 15 minute speech

About 1,950 to 2,250 words. Fifteen minutes is the standard slot at many conferences and the standard length for a TED style talk. It carries three or four main ideas, and it is usually the point where speakers start writing an essay and then wonder why the delivery feels heavy.

Why spoken words are not written words

Reading silently runs at 200 to 250 words per minute. Speaking runs at 130 to 150. A speaker who takes a 2,000 word essay to the podium has written an eight minute read and a fifteen minute talk, and the mismatch is why so many presentations finish early or get cut off.

Speech also spends words on things writing does not need. Signposting phrases such as as I said and here is the part that matters take time and add no information. That is normal in speech and clumsy in prose, which is why a transcript always looks padded.

If you want a page estimate, 1,400 words is roughly five and a half double spaced manuscript pages at 250 words per page, or about three single spaced pages. Our words to pages calculator handles both settings if you are printing a script.

How to time a draft without guessing

Read the opening aloud for exactly one minute at the pace you intend to use on the day, then count the words you got through. That number is your personal speaking rate, and it is usually lower than the rate you assume. Multiply by ten for a ten minute speech.

Then read the whole draft aloud once, with a timer, standing up. Reading silently is not a test, because your inner voice is faster than your mouth. If the timed read lands at nine minutes, you have room for one more example. If it lands at twelve, you need to cut, and no amount of speaking faster will fix a twelve minute script.

Cutting a draft that runs long

Cut by the paragraph rather than by the word. Removing adjectives from every sentence makes the whole thing thinner, while removing one paragraph removes a whole idea that the audience will not miss if it was supporting material rather than the argument.

A useful rule is the ten percent pass. Take your target length, multiply by 1.1 and treat that as the absolute ceiling. For ten minutes the ceiling is 1,540 words. Anything above it gets cut before rehearsal, not during it.

Use the character counter while you edit to see the word total update as you type, and check the paragraph count as well. A ten minute speech with forty paragraphs is a list of fragments. One with twelve is a talk.

Check the count before you rehearse. Paste your script into the character counter to see words and reading time, then divide by your own speaking rate rather than a generic one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words is a 10 minute speech?

About 1,300 to 1,500 words at a normal speaking pace of 130 to 150 words per minute. Plan for 1,400 words and keep some slack for pauses.

How many words for a 10 minute speech if I speak slowly?

Around 1,200 to 1,300. At 120 words per minute, ten minutes fits 1,200 words, and any script longer than that will need cuts rather than a faster delivery.

How many words is a ten minute speech in pages?

Roughly five and a half double spaced pages at 250 words per page, or about three single spaced pages. Large type and wide margins will push it closer to seven.

How many words is a 15 minute speech?

About 1,950 to 2,250 words. Plan for 2,100 words, which is the standard length for a conference slot or a TED style talk.

How many words is a 4 minute speech?

About 520 to 600 words. That is one short story plus one claim plus one piece of evidence, and very little room for a formal introduction.

How many words is a 2 minute speech?

About 260 to 300 words. Skip the introduction, tell one story and land the point, since there is no time to build an argument in stages.

How many words is an 8 minute speech?

About 1,040 to 1,200 words. Eight minutes comfortably holds three points with one example each, and starts to feel crowded at five points.

How many words per minute do people speak?

Conversation runs 150 to 170 words per minute and a formal presentation runs 130 to 150. Silent reading is faster, at 200 to 250, which is why scripts read faster than they speak.
