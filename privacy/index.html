★ Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to the text you paste into Bohuainter and what limited data the site collects.

The short version

Text you type or paste into any Bohuainter tool stays in your browser. It is counted by JavaScript running on your own device and it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it and no database that could store it.

Text you enter into the tools

All counting and conversion happens locally. Refreshing the page or closing the tab discards everything you typed. We do not use local storage or session storage to keep your text, and there is no autosave feature that could preserve it.

If you use a browser extension or a keyboard that records input, that is outside our control and outside this policy. Bohuainter itself does not record keystrokes.

Information we do collect

We collect standard web server logs when a page is requested. These contain the requested URL, the time of the request, the referring page, an approximate location derived from IP address and a browser user agent string. We use them to understand which tools are used and to detect abuse.

We use privacy respecting analytics to count page views in aggregate. We do not build individual profiles, we do not sell data and we do not combine analytics with any identifier that could point to a person.

Cookies

Bohuainter sets no cookies for the tools themselves. Advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure performance. Where required by law, a consent banner appears before any non essential cookie is set, and declining it does not restrict access to any tool.

Advertising

We display advertising to fund the site. Ad partners may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies. We do not pass your text, your email address or any identifier we hold to advertisers, because we do not hold such information in the first place.

Third party links and resources

Some pages load the Inter typeface from a public font service. That request reveals your IP address to that service in the ordinary way any web request does. Links to external sites are provided for reference and their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The tools are general purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the tools require no account and no personal information, there is nothing to delete on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not maintain user accounts and do not store content, in most cases we hold nothing that identifies you beyond short lived server logs. You can still write to us and we will explain what is held and remove what we can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your text is handled, we will update the date at the top of this page and describe the change in plain language. We will not quietly begin transmitting text that was previously counted locally.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to the address on the contact page. We answer privacy questions first.
