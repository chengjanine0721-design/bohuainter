Why writers use it

Counting is the easy part.
The hard part is staying in flow.

Most counters make you paste text into an ad covered page and squint at a number buried under a cookie banner. Bohuainter puts the number directly under the box you type in and updates it on every keystroke.

  • Your text never leaves the tab. Every count runs in JavaScript on your own machine.
  • Numbers you can act on. Pages, reading time and average sentence length, not just a total.
  • Formatted for real limits. Google title widths and X character rules are built in.

FAQ

Common questions

Do these tools send my text anywhere?

No. Every count runs in JavaScript inside your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored on a server.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There is no account, no trial and no character limit. The site is supported by unobtrusive advertising.

How accurate is the word count?

The counter splits text on whitespace, which matches how Microsoft Word and Google Docs count words. Hyphenated words count as one word.

Can I use these tools offline?

Once a page has loaded it keeps working if your connection drops, because there is no server call involved in the counting.
