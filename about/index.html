★ About us

A quiet corner of the internet for counting words

Bohuainter exists because checking a word count should not mean fighting a page full of pop ups.

What we do

We build small free tools that answer one question each: how many characters, how many words, how many pages, will this title fit. You open a page, paste your text, read a number and close the tab. That is the whole product.

Every tool runs in your browser. There is no account, no document library, no sync and no way for us to read what you type, because the text never travels anywhere.

Why we built it

Writers often need to know how many words or characters their text contains and whether it meets the requirements of an essay, a Facebook post, or a tweet. But getting a simple count isn't always as easy as it should be. You may need to switch between different tools, look up platform-specific limits, or search for a counter that gives you a reliable result.

Things get even more frustrating when different word counters show different results for the same text, some tools require you to sign up before using them, or you can't easily find the word count feature in Google Docs or Microsoft Word.

That's why we built this tool. We wanted to bring everything you need for word and character counting into one place: free, accurate, and easy to use, with no sign-up required.

Whether you're a student checking an essay, a teacher reviewing an assignment, or anyone writing content for the web, our goal is simple: help you count words and characters quickly, meet writing requirements with confidence, and make word counting easier.

Who it is for

  • Students checking an essay against a word limit
  • Writers trimming a draft to fit a submission guideline
  • Marketers writing titles and descriptions that survive Google's crop
  • Anyone who needs a character count for a form field and does not want to count manually

How we pay for it

The tools are free. The site is supported by unobtrusive advertising. No tool is gated, no feature is held back behind a subscription and there is no premium tier planned. If advertising ever stops covering the cost, we will say so plainly rather than slowly paywalling the counters.

What we will not do

We will not store your text. We will not sell data about what you paste. We will not add an AI feature that quietly uploads your draft. If a future tool cannot work without sending text to a server, it will say so on the page before you type a single character.

A tool that respects your attention is better than a tool that captures it.

Get in touch

Found a counting bug, want a tool that does not exist yet, or spotted a typo in a guide? The contact page is the fastest route, and we read every message.
